# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 November 2026

Hue Sort is published by OmegaAPI. This policy explains what the app does with data.

## What we collect

We do not ask for or store personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts, location, or account details. There is no signup and no cloud save.

Puzzle progress and settings stay on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Ads

The app uses Google AdMob to show interstitial and rewarded ads. AdMob may use advertising identifiers and device information to select and measure ads, subject to Google's own policies and your device settings. You can reset or limit advertising identifiers in Android settings.

Rewarded ads in Hue Sort unlock only in-game helpers: a hint, a board shuffle, or one extra tube. They never provide money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or other real-world value.

## Permissions

The app requests only:

- `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, used to load ads.

It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or notification permissions.

## Children

Hue Sort is rated **Everyone** and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to [info@omegaapi.com](mailto:info@omegaapi.com).
