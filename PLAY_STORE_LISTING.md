## Short description

A calm colour-sorting puzzle with hints and offline play.

## Full description

Hue Sort is a clean, offline-first colour puzzle. Move the top group of matching colours from one tube to another, sort every colour into its own tube, and enjoy a calm brain teaser that works without an account.

The board grows with you. Early levels use three colours; later levels add more colours, deeper tubes, and trickier layouts. Every puzzle is generated from a solved board using legal moves, so each level is solvable.

Optional ads keep the game free. Watch a short rewarded ad for a hint, a shuffle, or one extra tube. Interstitial ads appear only after completed levels, every three levels. Rewards are strictly in-game and never involve money, gift cards, or cryptocurrency.

No signup. No personal data collection. No social features. Just sort the colours and relax.
